AI isn't replacing IT jobs. It's rebranding them.

Meet **Senior AI Babysitter**, a small browser game where you supervise four AI agents trying to build one dark mode button. Feed them prompts, settle architecture arguments, catch imaginary APIs, and stop a simple toggle from becoming a microservices platform.

You have three minutes, two coffees, and a rapidly shrinking supply of patience. Ship the feature before the shift ends.

No signup, no API keys. Just a suspiciously familiar day at work.
