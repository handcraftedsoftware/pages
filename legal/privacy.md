This policy applies to handcrafted.software mobile apps that link to this page. These apps are designed with your privacy in mind.

#### Data Collection

We do not collect personal data through these apps. They do not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

#### Local Storage
App settings and saved content are stored locally on your device and are not sent to us. This data remains until it is replaced, cleared, or removed when you delete the app. Device backups may retain copies according to your Apple backup settings.

#### In-App Purchases
Some apps offer in-app purchases through Apple. Purchasing and restoring purchases require communication with Apple's services. The app uses purchase status information to unlock purchased features. We do not receive your payment card details or send your transaction information to our own servers.

Apple processes information under its own privacy policy:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

#### Changes
We may update this policy when our apps or privacy practices change. The latest version will be available on this page with an updated date.

#### Support
We are committed to respecting your privacy and providing you with a secure and seamless experience. If you have any questions about this policy, feel free to contact us.
