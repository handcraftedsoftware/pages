## Problem

Some things are too small for a spreadsheet, but still useful to track.

Tiny Tally gives you a quick way to count repeat moments, review patterns, and understand what is actually happening over time.

## How It Works

1. Create a tracker for the thing you want to count.
2. Log each event with a quick tap.
3. Review stats and trends by day, month, or year.
4. Export your data when you want to keep a copy elsewhere.

## Features

- **Custom trackers.** Create counters for habits, goals, routines, and personal patterns.
- **Fast logging.** Add a count quickly when something happens.
- **Stats and trends.** Review progress across days, months, and years.
- **Widgets and Watch support.** Use Home Screen widgets and Apple Watch support for quicker access.
- **Data export.** Export your tracking data when you need it.
- **On-device privacy.** Your data stays on your phone.

## Privacy

Tiny Tally keeps your data on your phone.

The app is designed as a small personal tracking utility without accounts or public sharing.

## Maker Note

I build small apps for focused real-world tasks. Tiny Tally exists for the moments that are easy to miss unless you can count them quickly.
