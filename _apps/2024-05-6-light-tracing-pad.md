## Problem

Sometimes you want to trace a sketch, reference photo, lettering sample, or small design without buying a separate lightbox.

Lightbox Tracing Pad gives you a clean, portable setup using the device you already have.

## Features

- **Import images quickly.** Choose a reference image from your device and prepare it for tracing.
- **Adjust for clearer lines.** Use filters and adjustments to make the image easier to see through paper.
- **Resize with touch.** Pinch and position the image so it fits your project.
- **Lock the screen while tracing.** Prevent accidental taps from moving the image while you draw.
- **Works on iPhone and iPad.** Use a portable tracing setup wherever you draw, craft, or practice.

## Privacy

Lightbox Tracing Pad does not collect data.

Your images stay on your device. The app is built as a small, focused utility without accounts or tracking.
