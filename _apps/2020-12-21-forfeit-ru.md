## What It Was

Новогодние фанты was built for New Year gatherings where a group wants a quick, silly prompt without preparing cards in advance.

The app had no winners or losers; the point was to keep the party moving with small tasks, songs, drawings, pantomime prompts, and group activities.

## How It Worked

1. Start the game during a New Year gathering.
2. Shake the phone to reveal a prompt.
3. Complete the task as a group or as one player.
4. Pass the phone to the next player.
