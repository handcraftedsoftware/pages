## Problem

Isometric ideas are easier to explore when the drawing surface already supports the right kind of grid.

Isometric Drawing gives you a focused canvas for practicing forms, sketching objects, and exploring axonometric views without opening a heavy design tool.

## How It Works

1. Open a focused isometric drawing canvas.
2. Use the grid to guide shapes and proportions.
3. Sketch ideas, objects, or simple visual concepts.
4. Try different axonometric projection types as needed.

## Features

- **Focused drawing canvas.** Work in a dedicated space for isometric drawing.
- **Grid-guided sketching.** Use the grid to keep shapes clean and consistent.
- **Axonometric projection options.** Explore different projection types.
- **Works on iPhone and iPad.** Draw on the device that fits your moment.
- **Privacy first.** The App Store privacy listing says the app does not collect data.

## Privacy

Isometric Drawing does not collect data.

The app is built as a focused drawing utility without account setup or tracking.
