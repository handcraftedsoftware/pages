## Problem

Loyalty cards are useful, but they are easy to forget, lose, or bury in a wallet.

CardsPack gives you one simple place to keep them on your phone, so the card you need is easier to find when you are paying.

## How It Works

1. Add your loyalty cards to CardsPack.
2. Keep them organized in one place.
3. Open the app when you need a card.
4. Show the right card at checkout.

## Features

- **One place for loyalty cards.** Keep cards together instead of searching through a wallet.
- **Quick access at checkout.** Open the app and find the card you need.
- **Simple focused design.** CardsPack is built for one practical everyday task.
- **Privacy first.** The App Store privacy listing says the app does not collect data.

## Privacy

CardsPack does not collect data.

The app is designed as a small utility for storing loyalty cards without account setup or tracking.
