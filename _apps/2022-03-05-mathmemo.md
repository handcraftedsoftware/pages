## What It Was

Pirate Math Memo was built as a simple learning game for practicing multiplication tables and addition questions.

The project combined familiar memory-card gameplay with math prompts, so practice could feel more like a small game than a worksheet.

## How It Worked

1. Choose a difficulty level.
2. Match cards in the memory game.
3. Answer arithmetic prompts during play.
4. Repeat rounds to practice multiplication and addition.
