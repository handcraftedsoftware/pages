Good to know

Who is Advent Adventure for?

Children and their families.

How does the calendar work?

Days 1–4 are free and always available. A one-time purchase unlocks the rest, with each window opening on its December date. Includes seven bonus days for December 25–31.

Do we need a printer?

Only for the paper crafts. Games, music and the tree work without one.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Games, music and printable PDFs are included in the app. Printing depends on your printer setup.

Which devices and language is it designed for?

The app is designed for iPhone and iPad, with activities in English.
