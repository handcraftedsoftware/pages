A few things you might wonder

Before you
roll away.

What will my child practise?

Finding and pressing the highlighted keys on an English QWERTY keyboard. The lessons introduce all 26 letter keys. The app is in English.

Do we need a physical keyboard?

Both work. Tap the on-screen keys, or practise on a physical keyboard with an English layout.

What’s included for free?

The first three app lessons are free. Full Access adds five more chapters, which open as earlier lessons are completed.

Can we play without an internet connection?

Yes, for unlocked lessons. Purchases and restoring access need internet. Progress stays on each device, without cloud sync.

What happens if they make a mistake?

Seven mistakes or getting caught ends an attempt. You can restart, or pause whenever you need a break.
