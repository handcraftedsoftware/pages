One productive week is a one-week A5 planner for balancing work, personal projects, home tasks, mood, health, and daily reflection.

Each day gives you space for three important work tasks, three personal project tasks, and three home tasks. There is also room to track mood, productivity, well-being, movement, reading, social connection, and one daily insight.

It is a practical tool for planning a focused week and noticing what helps you work well.
